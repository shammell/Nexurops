## Brand Guardrails

### Things That Sound "Off" in Your Voice:
- Using "stakeholders" (say "people involved")
- Saying "synergy" (say "collaboration")
- "In today's fast-paced world" (just say "today")
- Over-enthusiastic tone (you're direct, not salesy)

### Things That Sound "Right":
- "Here's what actually works..."
- "I was wrong about X, turns out Y..."
- "Try this instead of Y..."
- Specific numbers and examples
- Honest limitations ("This doesn't work for X")
